Key Terms

Learn these first. They come up constantly once you get into the actual screens.

Adoption Readiness Score

A 0–100 score showing how ready a farmer is to switch to organic farming. It's built from five weighted factors: Interest, Water availability, Chemical usage, Training received, and Participation. High is 70 or above, Medium is 40 to 69, Low is under 40.

Consulting Plan

Once a farmer converts (their plan becomes active or completed), they get a consulting plan with 4 checkpoints: Irrigation Advisory, Fertilizer Schedule, Spray Programme, and Crop-Stage Advisory. Each one moves from pending, to in-progress, to completed.

Territory & Geography

Every farmer's location follows four levels: State, District, Taluka, Village. A Taluka (also called a Tehsil) is a sub-district area — Maharashtra has 36 districts and around 355 talukas. Territory Assignment gives a rep a whole district; Territory Configuration marks exactly which villages a team covers.

Draft, Registered, Rejected

Draft: you started it but haven't submitted yet — only you can see it. Registered: submitted, and visible to your Team Lead for review. Rejected: your Team Lead sent it back with a reason — fix it and resubmit.

Sensitive Information

Aadhaar number, bank account details, and income. Hidden from Field Representatives and Data Entry Operators. Visible to Team Leads, Managers, and Leadership.

Extended Fields

Custom fields your Leadership or Team Lead can add to the registration form, on top of the standard ones. They show up later on the farmer's profile, under Additional Details.

Who Reports to Whom

Leadership is at the top and can do everything. Managers report to Leadership and handle platform-wide setup. Team Leads report to Managers and run one team. Field Representatives and Data Entry Operators report to a Team Lead and do the hands-on data capture.

GPS Photo Watermarking

When you take a photo during registration with the in-app camera, it gets stamped with your GPS location and the exact time. This proves the visit actually happened at the farmer's location.
